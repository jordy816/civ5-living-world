Civ V Living World - to play along
==================================
Civilization, played inside Minecraft. You join the game of whoever sent you this.

What you need
  - Minecraft: Java Edition (your own account).
  - Prism Launcher (free): https://prismlauncher.org  Install it and sign in with your Minecraft account.

Putting it in
  1. In Prism Launcher: Add Instance > Import > choose this zip file ("Civ V Living World.zip") > OK.
     It holds Minecraft 26.3 with Fabric, Fabric API and the mod. Prism fetches the game itself.
  2. Start the instance "Civ V Living World".

Joining
  3. The game first opens a world of its own. Leave it: Esc twice > Save and Quit to Title.
     Then Multiplayer > Direct Connection (or Add Server) > the address your host gives you.
     (If your host sent you a zip that goes straight to their server, skip this step.)
  4. No game yet? You sit at the same table as your host. First your host chooses the map
     and the rules (step 1 along the top); you watch. Then (step 2) pick a civilization on
     the left (point at one to see what is special about it) and click I AM READY. When
     everybody is, your host begins: you see a count of three.
     A game already running? You get a list of its civilizations: "Take over" one the computer
     plays, "Rule along" with somebody, or "Just look on".

Playing                                        (F1 in the game shows the mouse and the keys)
  You look down at the land as in a strategy game. Left click selects a unit or a city,
  right click sends the selected unit there. The big button bottom right always says what
  comes next; Enter presses it.
  Everybody moves at the same time. When you have ended your turn the button says
  WAITING FOR THE OTHERS. If you are at war with another player, the two of you move one after
  the other, and the button says who moves first.

  News is the small pictures down the right side: click one to go to where it happened,
  right-click to put it away.
  F12 sets you down on the land where your pointer is, on your own land only; F12 again and
  you are back above. On foot an enemy unit you come near sends its soldiers at you: each one
  you beat costs that unit health. If you fall you stand before your capital again, and it
  costs some gold. A governor sells better arms ("Arms for yourself").

Nothing has to be typed. Leaving: Esc > Disconnect. Your civilization is yours again when you
come back; while you are away the computer plays it so the others need not wait.
